Voxarc Community Guidelines

Effective: November 5, 2026

Voxarc Feed is a place for professionals to discuss policy and supply-chain signals. Keep it factual, useful and respectful. Breaking these rules can lead to removed content and account termination.

Do

  • Stay on topic: the signal, its sources and what it means for your work.
  • Cite sources when you make a factual claim.
  • Disagree with ideas, not people.

Do not post

  • Harassment, threats, bullying or hate based on identity
  • Defamatory remarks: false statements that harm a person or company
  • Abusive, sexual or violent content
  • Spam, advertising, scams or repeated self-promotion
  • Personal information about others, or confidential or insider information
  • Content that infringes copyright or breaks the law
  • Impersonation of people or organizations

Reporting and review

Use the menu on any comment or profile to report or block. A reported comment is hidden for everyone right away while Voxarc Lab reviews it, normally within 24 hours. Blocking someone hides their comments and profile from you immediately. To report an inaccurate or misleading signal, use the menu on the signal.

Enforcement

We may remove content, restore content found to be fine, suspend accounts, or terminate accounts for serious or repeated violations. Harassment, defamatory remarks and abusive content lead to account termination.

Acceptance

You agree to these guidelines when you create an account and before your first comment: "I agree to the Voxarc Community Guidelines and understand that harassment, defamatory remarks, and abusive content will lead to account termination."

Questions or appeals: support@voxarc.io.
