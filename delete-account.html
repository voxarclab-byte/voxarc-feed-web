Delete your account

Voxarc Feed is published by Voxarc Lab. Reading the app never needs an account. If you created one to comment, follow or sync, you can delete it here, in the app (Settings, Account, Delete account) or by email.

What is deleted
  • Your profile: name, handle and avatar
  • Your comments, follows and followers
  • Your reactions, saves and reposts
  • Your sign-in record (Google or email)

Deletion is permanent and done right away. Reports you filed are kept without your identity for up to 90 days for safety review. Content on your own device stays until you clear it or uninstall the app.
